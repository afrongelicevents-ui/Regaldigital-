Good questions.

How long does it take?

Most websites are ready in 3–7 days once we have your photos and details. Premium sites can take a little longer.

Why is there a monthly fee?

Every website needs hosting to stay online. The monthly fee covers hosting, security, backups, small updates and support.

Do I need to buy a domain?

No. Every site comes with a free web address. If you want your own .com or .co.zw, you buy it in your name and we connect it for free.

Can I change things later?

Yes. Small text and photo changes are included every month. New pages or a redesign are quoted separately.

What do I need to send you?

Your business name, logo if you have one, photos, services or products, prices and contact details.
